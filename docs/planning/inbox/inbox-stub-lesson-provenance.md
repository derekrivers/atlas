title: "Lesson.related_ticket_ids conflates provenance with citation: merge corrupts the source, the citation metric is permanently off by one"
objective: "A lesson's source ticket and its citation history are distinct stored fields, so provenance survives `lessons merge`, the citation count means what the report says it means, and the `--stale` and citation signals are computed from usage rather than from usage-plus-self."
context: "Live finding, 2026-07-17, during Phase 9 closure smoke. `atlas lessons report` displayed `Active lesson citations | Enforce ACTIVE-only lesson retrieval... | 1 | c41c0a43-...` for a lesson that has never been cited by any pack — c41c0a43 is ATLAS-65, the lesson's own SOURCE ticket. The field carries three meanings by position and no invariant protects any of them: (1) `atlas/learning/extractor.py:376` seeds `related_ticket_ids: [ticket.id]` at extraction — index 0 is PROVENANCE; (2) `atlas/pm/sync.py`'s `_record_lesson_citation_feedback` (ATLAS-101) APPENDS completing tickets — indices 1..N are USAGE; (3) `atlas/cli.py:2512` `_source_ticket_label` reads `related_ticket_ids[0]` and labels it `source=` — correct only by positional luck; (4) `atlas/learning/report.py:182` computes `citation_count=len(lesson.related_ticket_ids)` — so every ACTIVE lesson is born with a phantom citation of itself, permanently; (5) `atlas lessons merge` (ATLAS-100) UNIONS the two lists — after any merge, index 0 is whichever lesson won and the other lesson's source silently becomes a 'citation'. Provenance is destroyed by a supported operation. The pending 153/158 merge ruling is exactly that case. learning-system.md is itself ambiguous and must be fixed with the code: its extraction section says a lesson is produced with `related_ticket_ids` populated, while its retrieval-interplay section says citation ADDS the completing ticket to `related_ticket_ids` — one field, two documented purposes. Pre-ruled decisions: D-1 add `Lesson.source_ticket_id: UUID` (single, immutable, set at extraction) and redefine `related_ticket_ids` as citations-only; the extractor stops seeding it. D-2 migration (next number in sequence) backfills `source_ticket_id = related_ticket_ids[0]` for every existing row and removes that element from `related_ticket_ids`, so the 18 backfilled lessons keep correct provenance and drop their phantom citation; the migration is data-preserving and reversible in the usual house form. D-3 `_source_ticket_label` reads `source_ticket_id`; the `source=` display is unchanged for the operator. D-4 `report.py`'s citation count becomes `len(related_ticket_ids)` under the new definition — an uncited ACTIVE lesson reports 0. D-5 `lessons merge` preserves the TARGET's `source_ticket_id` unchanged and unions only citations; the merged-away lesson's source is recorded in the merge's audit trail, not silently promoted to a citation. D-6 `--stale` (ATLAS-100) and any pack-count signal are recomputed against citations-only; confirm no other consumer reads the field positionally. D-7 learning-system.md's extraction and retrieval-interplay sections are corrected in the same change to name the two fields separately; the schema export and its enumeration pin are updated as a named change."
